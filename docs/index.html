Each colored stop's shade is based on its on-time / early / late counts across every visit in the window above. On time means at least 85% of those visits landed within GoCary's on-time definition (1 min early to 5 min late). Below that, the stop is colored by which way it's skewing: trending late if more visits were late than early, trending early otherwise. Not enough data means fewer than 5 visits have been recorded yet — too small a sample to call either way. "Show all stops" adds every other known stop in gray, with no color logic applied — most of those will stay gray permanently, since GoCary's real-time feed only reports live delay predictions at schedule-checkpoint ("timepoint") stops, a subset of each route's full stop list; a gray dot there usually means this stop never gets live data at all, not that it's still waiting on one.
